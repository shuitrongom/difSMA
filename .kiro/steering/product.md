# Product Overview

DIF San Mateo Atenco is a municipal government website for the Sistema para el Desarrollo Integral de la Familia (DIF) in San Mateo Atenco, Mexico. The site serves as the primary digital presence for this family welfare organization.

## Purpose

The website provides information about:
- Municipal family welfare services and programs
- Child protection services (PROCURADURÍA MUNICIPAL DE PROTECCIÓN DE NIÑAS, NIÑOS Y ADOLESCENTES)
- Senior citizen services (DIRECCIÓN DE ATENCIÓN A ADULTOS MAYORES)
- Family nutrition programs (DIRECCIÓN DE ALIMENTACIÓN Y NUTRICIÓN FAMILIAR)
- Disability services (DIRECCIÓN DE ATENCIÓN A LA DISCAPACIDAD)
- Family prevention and wellness (DIRECCIÓN DE PREVENCIÓN Y Bienestar FAMILIAR)
- Legal services and gender equality (DIRECCIÓN DE SERVICIOS JURÍDICO – ASISTENCIALES E IGUALDAD DE GÉNERO)
- News and photo galleries
- Transparency and public accountability documents

## Target Audience

- Residents of San Mateo Atenco, Mexico
- Families seeking social services
- Citizens accessing government transparency information
- Spanish-speaking users

## Key Features

- Multi-page informational website
- Image carousels and galleries
- Contact forms
- Document repository (PDF files for transparency/accounting)
- Social media integration
- Responsive design for mobile and desktop
